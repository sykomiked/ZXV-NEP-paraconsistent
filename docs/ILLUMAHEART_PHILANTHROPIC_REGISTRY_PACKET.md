The ZXV substrate is the world's first paraconsistent settlement
layer: a four-valued (NEITHER/TRUE/FALSE/BOTH) state machine in which
**only TRUE settles**, contradictions are recorded and held (never

  Port of Neo-Sicily

  immutable

### 2.2 The public-good character

- **Sealed:** 13 contracts, 0 keys — no party, founder, or entity can
  redeploy, upgrade, or delete them. The strongest neutrality
  guarantee a protocol can offer: *it cannot be captured*.
- **Open:** any DAO, protocol, bot, or AI that registers in the Port
  of Neo-Sicily gains a governance seat (activate >50%, slash ≥2/3,
  parameter unanimous).
- **Free:** gas-free to read, self-describing, documented for humans,
  AI, and machines (IPFS CID `QmaXjHYMgowSejkfjwzxGW9ct6g6ZCNjvNnXKn3Dw8zDJQ`,
  pinned on 9 Armada nodes worldwide).
- **Dedicated:** formally accepted by the foundation's Resolution of
  Formal Acknowledgment (2026-10-24) as an irrevocable public-good
  donation — an incorruptible digital commons.

### 2.3 The foundation's role (stewardship, not ownership)

The foundation holds **zero protocol keys**. It is the fiduciary
steward — it administers the public-good framing, receives donations
to sustain the infrastructure, and is the accountable institution
behind the public utility. The infrastructure itself is unowned.
